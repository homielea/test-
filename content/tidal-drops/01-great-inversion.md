## 2. Written post

On the Galician coast, a machine can weave a fishing net in an hour. Perfect knots. Costs almost nothing.

And still, every morning, the redeiras sit on the quay — the net-menders, a trade older than the harbor wall.

The machines never replaced them, because their skill was never the knot.

Their skill is reading the net.

This tear came from rock: patch it. This one came from strain: it will spread and take the whole panel with it. And this net is finished — no morning of mending will save it, and a good redeira says so before you waste the day.

Economics has one quiet rule: when something becomes abundant, value moves to whatever stays scarce beside it.

Knots are abundant now. Drafts, code, designs, plans — all knots. Tied faster and cleaner than our hands ever managed.

What stayed scarce is the reading. Which tear matters. Which one spreads. Which net you stop mending.

Anyone can tie the knot.

The work is knowing which holes matter.
