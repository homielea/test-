# Tidal Drop 02 — Tidal Planning / The Content Tide System

**Seed:** Rhythm over schedule. Plan by tide, not by calendar.
**Surface:** Attention Alchemist (faceless)

---

## 1. Short-form video script (faceless, ~170 words spoken)

[visual: fishing boats resting on wet mud, low tide, slack ropes, gulls]

Twice a day, this harbor empties. The boats sit down in the mud and wait.

No one here calls that lost time.

[visual: close on weathered hands mending a net]

The fishermen don't fight the tide. They plan by it. Nets get mended at slack water. Boats go out on the flood. The sea sets the schedule, and the work arranges itself around the sea.

Somewhere, we decided output should be constant. Post daily. Ship weekly. Same energy every morning — as if the water would never dare to breathe.

[visual: flat calendar grid, every square filled, slowly dissolving into moving water]

But attention moves like water. It rises. It slackens. Forcing high tide at all hours doesn't make more ocean. It just teaches you to distrust your own water.

[visual: the tide flooding back in over the harbor floor, boats lifting]

So plan by tide, not by calendar. High water: make. Slack water: mend — notes, walks, sharpening.

The tide always comes back.

Your job isn't to force it. It's to be ready when it does.

## 2. Written post (X / LinkedIn)

At slack tide, the fishermen mend nets.

Nobody stands at the harbor wall demanding the water come back faster.

Somewhere, work decided to ignore this. Constant output. A flat calendar. The same energy every day, as if we were machines and not weather.

Biology has one quiet rule: everything alive moves in pulses. Heartbeats. Seasons. Tides. A flat line is what the monitor shows when something has died.

So plan by tide, not by calendar.

High water: make the thing.
Slack water: mend — read, walk, sort the notes, sharpen the tools.
Low tide: look at the seabed. See what the busyness was hiding.

The output doesn't shrink. It concentrates.

The tide always comes back. Being ready is the whole job.
